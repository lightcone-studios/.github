# Security Policy

We take reports seriously and we appreciate the people who make them.

## Reporting a vulnerability

- **Preferred:** use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on the affected repository (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability"), where available.
- **Otherwise:** open an issue on the affected repository saying only that you have a security report and need a private channel. Do not include details in the public issue. We will respond with a private contact route.

## What to expect

- Acknowledgment within 72 hours.
- An assessment and remediation plan, with credit to the reporter if desired, once the issue is resolved.
- No legal action against good-faith research conducted without data exfiltration, service disruption, or privacy violations.

## Scope notes

Repositories handling personal information (for example, Builders Index) are held to a higher bar. Reports affecting those are prioritized first.
